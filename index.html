Gauze that shows when something changes.

WoundCue is sterile, absorbent gauze with built-in pH- and temperature-sensitive indicators. It gives you a visual cue to watch as your wound heals at home.

Once you leave the clinic, you’re the one watching the wound.

After surgery or an injury, people are usually responsible for changing dressings and monitoring the wound on their own. It can be hard to know whether healing looks normal or whether it’s time to call someone.

Traditional gauze protects a wound, but it can’t tell you anything about what’s happening underneath. WoundCue adds a simple visual signal to a product people already use, so there’s one more thing to go on and less guesswork.

How WoundCue works

No apps, no extra equipment, no medical training.

  1. Cover the wound

    Apply WoundCue like any sterile gauze pad. It absorbs fluid and protects the area.

  2. Check the indicators

    Built-in indicators respond to changes in pH and temperature. If the environment changes, you’ll see the color shift.

  3. Know when to call

    Instructions in every box explain what each color means and when to contact a healthcare professional.

Who WoundCue is for

Post-surgical patients

People recovering at home who want an extra way to keep an eye on an incision between appointments.

Parents and caregivers

Anyone looking after a child, partner, or parent who needs a simple way to track a wound without medical know-how.

Older adults and people with chronic wounds

People who need ongoing wound care and benefit from regular visual checks. We’ll test suitability carefully for this group.

Athletes and active people

Cuts, scrapes, and minor injuries that need convenient, reliable protection.

Important: what WoundCue is not

WoundCue does not diagnose infection and does not replace a healthcare professional. The indicators are designed to show that something about the wound environment has changed.

If you’re worried about a wound, or you see a color change, contact your doctor or other healthcare provider. A lack of color change doesn’t mean a wound is healing normally.

WoundCue is still in development, and regulatory requirements are being determined. No product is available for purchase yet.

Questions

Can I buy WoundCue today?

Not yet. We’re in development and plan to launch through pharmacies in 2027. This website is for information only.

Does a color change mean my wound is infected?

No. A color change means the pH or temperature around the dressing has shifted. Only a healthcare professional can tell you whether a wound is infected. If you see a change, follow the instructions in the box and contact your provider.

Will it replace my regular dressings?

WoundCue works like standard sterile gauze. Always follow the wound care instructions you were given by your doctor or nurse.

What technology does it use?

We’re adapting thin-film sensor technology originally developed by NASA into pH- and temperature-sensitive indicators for gauze. We’re working through licensing and testing, and we’ll share more as it’s confirmed.

Where will I be able to find it?

In the first-aid aisle at participating pharmacies and drugstores, and on their online stores. We’re starting with independent and regional pharmacies and plan to expand from there.
